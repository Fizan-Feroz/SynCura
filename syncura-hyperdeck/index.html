Methodology.

Dataset & PreprocessingLAB—04 / PHYSIONET 2012
01
Source
4,000 ICU patients (age ≥16), 37 time-series variables over 48h, binary mortality outcome.
02
Features
12 total — HR, RespRate, Temp, SysBP, DiasBP, SpO2, GCS, BUN, Creatinine, WBC, Platelets, Glucose.
6→12
03
Window
90-minute sliding windows, 15-minute stride, proximity labeling over the last 12h of stay.
60→90 min
04
Split
Patient-level 80/20 via GroupShuffleSplit — prevents leakage across windows of one patient.

Training StrategyLAB—04 / 30+ CONFIGS
01
Optimizer
Adam, learning rate 3e-4.
02

03
